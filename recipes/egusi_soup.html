Egusi Soup

DESCRIPTION

Egusi soup is a thick Nigerian soup made from ground melon seeds, rich in protein and commonly eaten with swallow.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup ground egusi (melon seeds)
  • 1/2 cup palm oil
  • 2 cups spinach or bitter leaf
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cups meat or fish stock
  • Assorted meat or fish
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Boil meat with seasoning until tender.
  2. Heat palm oil and fry onions.
  3. Add egusi and fry lightly.
  4. Add stock and cooked meat.
  5. Simmer until thick.
  6. Add vegetables and cook for 5 minutes.
  7. Adjust seasoning.
  8. Serve with pounded yam or eba.
