Jollof Rice

DESCRIPTION:

Jollof rice is a rich, flavorful West African dish cooked in a spiced tomato sauce. It’s vibrant, slightly smoky, and a party favorite across Nigeria.

  • 2 cups long grain parboiled rice
  • 5 large tomatoes
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • Salt to taste

STEP:

  1. Blend tomatoes, peppers, and half onion until smooth.
  2. Heat oil and fry chopped onions.
  3. Add tomato paste and fry for 3–5 minutes.
  4. Pour in blended mixture and cook until thick.
  5. Add stock, seasoning, thyme, curry, and salt.
  6. Add washed rice and stir.
  7. Cover and cook on low heat until rice is soft.
  8. Stir and serve hot.
