Ogbono Soup

DESCRIPTION:

Ogbono soup is a smooth, drawy soup made from wild mango seeds. It has a unique texture and rich taste.

INGREDIENTS:

  • 1 cup ground ogbono seeds
  • 1/2 cup palm oil
  • 2 cups meat or fish stock
  • Assorted meat or fish
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp crayfish2
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • Salt to taste

STEPS:

  1. Cook meat with seasoning until soft.
  2. Heat palm oil (do not bleach).
  3. Add ogbono and stir continuously.
  4. Pour in stock and mix well.
  5. Add meat, crayfish, and seasoning.
  6. Simmer until thick and drawy.
  7. Adjust salt.
  8. Serve with any swallow.
